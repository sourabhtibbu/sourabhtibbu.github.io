Nebula Swarm privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Nebula Swarm is a game that runs on your device. It does not collect, sell or share your personal information.

What the game keeps, and where

  • Your pilot name and age. The game asks for these the first time you play. They are saved only on your device, in the game's own storage. Your age is used only to suggest a starting difficulty. Neither is ever sent to us or anyone else.
  • Game progress. Save slots, coins, ships you own, upgrades, settings and the local scoreboard are also saved only on your device.

To delete all of this, uninstall the game, or clear the app's storage (Android: Settings → Apps → Nebula Swarm → Storage → Clear storage).

What the game does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No analytics, and no selling or sharing of data.
  • No in-app purchases. Coins are earned only by playing.

Files loaded from other services

The first time it starts, the game downloads its 3D graphics library from cdnjs (run by Cloudflare) and its lettering from Google Fonts. As with any download, those services can see your device's internet address when the files are fetched. After that, the game keeps copies on your device and can run offline.

Device features

If you turn on motion steering, the game reads your device's tilt only while you play, to move your ship. Game controllers are read only to control the game. Neither is recorded or sent anywhere.

Children

Nebula Swarm is suitable for players of all ages. Because nothing is collected or sent from the device, no personal information from children leaves the device.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, use the support email listed on the game's Google Play or Microsoft Store page.
